### 12. Forward and Backward Computation in a Neural Network (14 points)

Consider a neural network with one hidden layer and no bias. The input is

$$
x = [2,1,1]^T
$$

The hidden layer uses ReLU, the output layer also uses ReLU, and the loss is

$$
L(y,t)=\frac{1}{2}(y-t)^2
$$

The weights are

$$
W =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & -1 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
V = [3 \quad -1]
$$

and the target is

$$
t=2
$$

Do the following:

(1) Compute the hidden representation $h$ and the output $y$. (4 points)

(2) Compute the loss $L$. (2 points)

(3) Compute

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial V}
$$

and

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial W}
$$

by backpropagation. (6 points)

(4) Is there a dead hidden neuron in this example? Explain briefly. (2 points)

### 13. Reinforcement Learning (14 points)

Consider an episodic MDP with discount factor

$$
\gamma = 0.9
$$

There are two nonterminal states $s_1$ and $s_2$, and one terminal state $T$.

At $s_1$, policy $\pi$ chooses action $a$ with probability $0.7$ and action $b$ with probability $0.3$.

Transitions:

- From $s_1$ with action $a$: go to $s_2$ with reward $2$.
- From $s_1$ with action $b$: go to $T$ with reward $0$.
- At $s_2$, there is only one action $c$, which goes to $T$ with reward $3$.

Do the following:

(1) Compute $v_{\pi}(s_2)$ and $v_{\pi}(s_1)$ using the Bellman equation. (6 points)

(2) Suppose the current action-value estimates are

$$
Q(s_1,a)=1.2,\quad Q(s_2,c)=2.0,\quad Q(s_2,d)=1.5
$$

and a transition

$$
(s_1,a,r=2,s_2)
$$

is observed. Assume that under the current behavior policy, the next action actually chosen at $s_2$ is $d$. Let

$$
\alpha = 0.1,\quad \gamma = 0.9
$$

Compute one Sarsa update and one Q-learning update for $Q(s_1,a)$. (4 points)

(3) Explain in one short paragraph why the two updated values in (2) are different. (4 points)

### 14. Transformer Positional Encoding (8 points)

In sinusoidal positional encoding, for a fixed dimension pair indexed by frequency $\omega$, define

$$
PE_{pos} =
\begin{bmatrix}
\sin(\omega pos) \\
\cos(\omega pos)

Show that for any fixed offset $k$, there exists a $2 \times 2$ matrix $M_k$ such that

$$
PE_{pos+k}=M_k PE_{pos}
$$

Write down $M_k$ explicitly and explain why this property is useful.
